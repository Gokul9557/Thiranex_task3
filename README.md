# # Business Sales Analysis and Forecasting Using Power BI

## Thiranex Internship – Task 3

### Introduction
This project presents a business intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI Desktop to evaluate sales performance and predict future sales patterns.

By analyzing historical transactional data, the project provides a comprehensive understanding of revenue generation, profitability, product performance, and regional sales contributions.

### Project Goals
- Examine historical sales data to understand business performance.
- Identify revenue and profitability trends.
- Evaluate sales distribution across product categories and regions.
- Build interactive reports with dynamic filtering.
- Implement sales forecasting to support future business planning.

### Technology Stack
| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Power BI Desktop | Dashboard creation and reporting |
| Power Query | Data cleaning and transformation |
| DAX | Business metric calculations |
| Power BI Analytics | Sales forecasting |
| CSV Dataset | Historical sales data source |

### Data Source
**Superstore Sales Dataset – Kaggle**

The dataset includes information related to customer orders, sales revenue, profit, product categories, shipping dates, and regional business operations.

### Development Process

#### Phase 1: Data Import and Transformation
- Imported the CSV dataset into Power BI.
- Validated date, numeric, and categorical columns.
- Removed duplicate Row ID entries.
- Filtered missing sales values.
- Applied transformations to prepare the dataset for analysis.

#### Phase 2: Business Metrics
Developed DAX measures for the following performance indicators:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Overall sales revenue |
| Total Profit | Total profit generated |
| Total Orders | Number of unique orders |
| Profit Margin | Profit as a percentage of sales |

#### Phase 3: Data Visualization
Created a dashboard with multiple analytical components:

**Sales Trend Analysis**

A line chart representing sales performance over time to identify historical trends.

**Category Performance**

A clustered bar chart comparing revenue across different product categories.

**Regional Distribution**

A donut chart illustrating the contribution of different regions to total sales.

**KPI Summary**

Interactive cards displaying important business performance measures.

**Date-Based Filtering**

A slicer enabling users to explore sales performance across selected periods.

#### Phase 4: Sales Forecasting
Implemented the forecasting functionality available in Power BI to estimate future sales performance based on historical trends.

Forecast settings:
- Forecast Horizon: 6 Months
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Seasonality: Auto

### Key Deliverables
- Cleaned and structured sales dataset
- DAX-based business performance metrics
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Historical sales analysis
- Future sales forecast visualization
- Power BI project file (.pbix)

The project highlights the importance of business intelligence and predictive analytics in understanding historical sales performance and supporting future decision-making. Microsoft Power BI provides an effective platform for converting transactional data into meaningful business insights.
